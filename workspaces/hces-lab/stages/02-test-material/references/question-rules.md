# Question Rules

## Good Questions

- Ask what a reader would ask: a character's motive, where an event happened, how a theme comes back.
- Each answerable question needs specific passages, not general knowledge of the book.
- Vary how many gold passages a question needs: one, two to three, many.

## Unanswerable Questions

- Plausible for the book, but the text truly does not answer them.
- The right system reply is "not in the documents".

## Needles

- Short invented passages, written in the volume's style, inserted between real passages.
- Each needle is the only place its question is answered.
- Spread needles across the start, middle and end of each volume.

## Hard Cases

- Low overlap: the answer shares few words with the question.
- Lookalikes: passages that use the question's words but do not answer it.

## Known Bias

Famous volumes may be in model training data, so catch rates on real passages can look better than they are. Needles give the cleanest numbers.
