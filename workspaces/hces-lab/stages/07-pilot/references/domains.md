# Pilot Domains

Two made-up workflows, written as the user's own description for workspace-builder stage 01. No private data.

## podcast-episode

I run a weekly interview podcast and want a workspace that takes an episode from booking to published show notes. It starts with a guest name and a topic. First I research the guest: past interviews, their book or company, anything they have said publicly about the topic, and I want a one-page guest brief. Then I write an interview outline with an opening, 8 to 12 questions in three blocks, and a closing; I always want to review and reorder the questions before recording. After recording I get a transcript from Descript as a text export. From the transcript I need a cut list (timestamps of tangents, false starts and ad breaks), then show notes with a summary, chapter markers with timestamps, links mentioned, and a pull quote. Last, three social posts: one for LinkedIn, one for X, one for Instagram, each in the show's voice. The show's name, host name, voice rules and sponsor read change if someone else uses this, and the sponsor read is optional because some episodes have no sponsor. Descript is the only tool; ffmpeg is optional for making audio clips. Every episode, the guest, topic and recording date are new.

## small-grant

I help small community groups apply for local foundation grants of 5,000 to 50,000 dollars. The workspace should go from a funder's call for proposals to a submission-ready application. Input is the call (a PDF or web page I paste in as text) plus the group's own notes. First I extract the funder's requirements: eligibility, deadline, budget limits, required sections, word limits and scoring criteria, into a checklist. Then I check the group against eligibility and stop if they do not qualify. Next I draft a project narrative section by section, matched to the scoring criteria, in plain language; the group must approve the narrative before anything else. Then a line-item budget and a budget justification that stay under the funder's cap, and a short evaluation plan with two or three measurable outcomes. Last, a final compliance pass that checks every required section, word limit and attachment against the checklist. What varies by group: organization name, mission statement, service area, past grants, and the board chair who signs. A letter of support step is optional, only when the funder asks for one. LibreOffice is needed to export the final document to PDF and Word. Each run has a new funder, deadline and project.
